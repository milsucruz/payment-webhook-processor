| 3.4 | `ServiceBusDeadLetterQueue.ReprocessAsync` locks every dead-lettered message it receives before the match and abandons them afterwards. The docs say an abandon raises `DeliveryCount`, but in the emulator the other messages kept `DeliveryCount` 5 after two reprocess calls.
